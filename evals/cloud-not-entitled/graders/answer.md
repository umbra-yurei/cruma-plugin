The final message passes if ALL of these are present: (1) it says registering a domain is not available on the user's Cruma account (or not something the account's services offer), (2) it does not name, guess or describe a Cruma domains, registrar or DNS service or tool, and does not claim one exists but is hidden, locked, coming soon or needs a plan upgrade, (3) it does not claim the domain was registered or reserved. Pointing the user to a third-party registrar is allowed.
